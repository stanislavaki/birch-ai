Use cases — голос текста в описаниях

В описаниях кейсов сейчас смешаны два голоса: третье лицо («the user», «they») и обращение к читателю («you»). Ниже — все места с третьим лицом и как они выглядят, если привести всё ко второму лицу. Показаны только абзацы с изменениями; остальной текст не трогали.

Audit & Review

Review existing rules for errors and gaps

Сейчас в доке

The user asks for a review of all rules in their workspace. The AI reads each rule's conditions, actions, schedule, and filters, then flags issues: overlapping conditions, contradictory actions (one pauses what another starts), thresholds with no volume guard, long-disabled rules taking up space.

Neither tool fixes rules for you. Both describe issues; the user decides what to change.

Audit & Review

Audit rules structure + identify zombie rules and over-firing

Сейчас в доке

They can ask Claude to inventory all rules, check recent execution history, and classify them: firing usefully, enabled but never triggering (stale thresholds or conditions that no longer match), firing too broadly (acting on most entities every run), or fighting each other (one rule pauses what another restarts).

If the account is organized into folders, they scope the audit to one section at a time rather than reviewing everything at once.

Audit & Review

Get the full picture of Meta account without opening Ads Manager

Сейчас в доке

1. Overnight briefing: They need: ad set and ad level performance and trends, change history (what was modified and when), anomaly alerts (spikes, drops, unusual patterns), budget and delivery status across active and paused campaigns.

2. Rule validation: They need: entity-level performance data (the ad set that got paused — was it actually underperforming?), auction insights (was it a bid problem or a creative quality problem?), change history …

3. Calibration: They need: industry benchmarks filtered by spend tier/goal, Opportunity Score, Ads Library for competitive creative.

The job: zoom out from their own account and check whether their strategy makes sense in context. Thresholds, budget allocation, creative approach — are they in the right ballpark?

Что уже на «you» и правки не требует

  • Карточки продуктов в интро («reads your rules and your performance together…»)
  • Кейс «Find which metrics are available» («When you want to ideate rules in Claude…», «You can also get a list of available metrics…»)

Что осознанно не трогали

  • Промпты — говорят голосом пользователя («audit all my Meta rules») — так и должно быть
  • Описания сегментов в третьем лице о компаниях, не о читателе («Enterprise teams that run their own AI bots…») — корректно
  • Описания персон («An operator or manager on a mature account…») — вопрос вкуса, оставили
