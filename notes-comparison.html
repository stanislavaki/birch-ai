Audit & Review убрать

Открытый баг, а не граница продукта. На странице про аудит правил читается как «наш аудит может молча соврать» — и станет неправдой, когда баг починят.

Сейчас
BïrchAI BïrchMCP

Audit rules structure + identify zombie rules and over-firing

An operator or manager on a mature account where rules accumulated over months. Often triggered by unexplained performance instability, a budget spike, or an account handover: "I have 80 rules and I've lost track — what's actually running, and is anything working against me?"

Ask Claude to inventory all rules, check recent execution history, and classify them: firing usefully, enabled but never triggering, firing too broadly, or fighting each other. If your account is organized into folders, scope the audit to one section at a time.

list_rule_folders→list_automation_rules→list_rule_logs→get_rule_log

list_automation_rules currently only returns rules in the "General" category — rules in other categories are invisible to the MCP, so the audit may be incomplete.

Monitor & Control убрать

Дефекта нет — у Birch MCP другая специализация. Но это уже сказано дважды: бейджем Bïrch AI на карточке и списком в карточке продукта вверху. Отсутствие бейджа MCP говорит то же самое, без отрицания.

Сейчас
BïrchAI

Quick performance overview

"How is my ROAS across all ad accounts this week vs last week?" or "Compare spend for Meta and TikTok for Q1." Birch AI answers in natural language, supports custom timeframes, and works across all connected platforms at the ad account level. Entity-level breakdown is Meta only and limited to accounts with a manageable entity count.

Birch MCP cannot do this — it has no performance metrics.

Build & Set Up смягчить

«Meta only» звучит дважды — на бейдже и в заметке. Убираем с бейджа (в тексте границу заодно смягчаем), бейджи выравниваются. «Always review drafts» — место в продукте, при создании черновика, а не на странице, где оно заранее сеет недоверие.

Create a rule from natural language

You describe what you want: "pause my ad sets if spend is over 100 for the last 3 days and cost per purchase is above 40." Birch AI creates a draft rule, saved to the "Birch AI Playbook" folder. You review, edit if needed, and activate.

Draft generation is Meta only — TikTok, Snapchat, and Google get text descriptions of the rule logic. Always review drafts before activating.
