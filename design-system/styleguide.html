The AI-chat mockup used inside use-case accordions and promo blocks. .chat-card-wrap owns the sizing and declares container-type: inline-size; every dimension inside the card is em/cqi, so the whole thing scales from one font-size. Set the wrapper's height (or width) and nothing inside needs touching.
